## Data treatment

`hardness_data.csv` contains nine spatially separated Rockwell hardness indentations (H1–H9) for each processing condition S1–S12. The revised figure reports the condition mean with **±1 sample standard deviation (SD), n = 9**.

These nine measurements quantify **within-condition spatial hardness variability**. They should not be described as nine independently fabricated specimen replicates.

The computed SD values are approximately 0.31–0.69 HRB, corresponding to coefficients of variation of about 1.7–3.3% across the twelve conditions.

## Interpretation boundary

The revised discussion should distinguish measured evidence from mechanism:

- densification is a bulk consolidation descriptor and is not a monotonic predictor of hardness;
- grain refinement can increase resistance to dislocation motion, but the optical grain-size response is non-monotonic;
- GNP/dislocation obstruction and thermal-mismatch strengthening are physically plausible, but are not directly confirmed without TEM;
- improved GNP dispersion can enhance reinforcement efficiency, whereas clustering can reduce it, but uniform GNP dispersion is not claimed without spatial SEM–EDS/TEM evidence.
